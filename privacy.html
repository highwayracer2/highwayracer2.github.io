Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026. Your privacy is paramount to us at Highway Racer 2 (highwayracer2.github.io). This policy explains our data practices and how we protect players of all ages.

1. Overview & Commitment

Highway Racer 2 provides free online browser-based video games. We do not require account registration, usernames, credit cards, or passwords to play any games on our portal. You can enjoy all features anonymously.

2. Information We Collect

A. Log Files & Technical Information

Like most standard website servers, we automatically log certain non-personally identifiable technical information when you access our site:

  • Browser type and version (e.g. Chrome, Firefox, Safari)
  • Operating system (e.g. Windows, macOS, Android, iOS)
  • Referring/exit pages and timestamps
  • Screen resolution and device category (desktop, tablet, mobile) for rendering optimization

B. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser cookies and HTML5 LocalStorage solely to enhance your gameplay experience, such as saving your high scores, unlocked vehicles, volume preferences, and game progression directly in your local browser cache.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Vendors (Google AdSense)

We partner with Google AdSense to serve advertisements when you visit our website. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site:

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Protecting the privacy of young children is especially critical. Highway Racer 2 strictly adheres to COPPA guidelines:

  • We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
  • All games on our portal are vetted for family suitability and child safety.
  • If a parent or guardian discovers that their child has provided personal information without consent, please contact us immediately at contact@highwayracer2.github.io, and we will promptly delete such data from our records.

5. GDPR & CCPA/CPRA Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the European Economic Area or California), you hold specific statutory rights regarding personal data:

  • Right to Know & Access: You may request confirmation of whether any data is processed.
  • Right to Deletion: You have the right to request deletion of your data.
  • Right to Opt-Out: We do NOT sell or share personal consumer information for monetary consideration.

6. Contact for Privacy Inquiries

If you have questions, feedback, or data requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our designated privacy compliance team:

Email: contact@highwayracer2.github.io
Website: highwayracer2.github.io
