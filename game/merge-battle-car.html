ABOUT MERGE BATTLE CAR

Merge Battle Car combines strategic vehicle merging mechanics with futuristic arena battle racing! Combine matching vehicles to unlock higher-tier battle cars equipped with plasma blasters and heavy armor.

Deploy your upgraded fleet into the combat arena, destroy enemy vehicles, and collect coins to dominate the leaderboard!

Key Features

  • Vehicle Merge Gameplay: Drag and combine identical cars to create super battle vehicles.
  • Automated Arena Battles: Send your battle cars into combat to generate passive income.
  • Futuristic Cyber Fleet: Unlock dozens of unique armored sports cars and battle tanks.

HOW TO PLAY & CONTROLS GUIDE

Keep merging lower-tier cars to maintain high-level battle cars on your main arena track.

Controls & Keybindings

  • Select & Merge Vehicles: Left Mouse Drag & Drop.
  • Start Battle / Speed Up: Spacebar or Click Speed Button.

Pro Tips

  • Clear empty garage spots frequently so new delivery crates can drop in automatically!
