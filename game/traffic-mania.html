ABOUT TRAFFIC MANIA

Traffic Mania is a high-octane driving and vehicle game available to play free online directly in your browser with zero downloads. Control busy city intersections, switch traffic lights, and direct vehicle flows safely to prevent catastrophic road collisions.

Put your driving instincts to the test, master challenging routes, and compete for top leaderboards on Highway Racer 2.

  • Dynamic intersection traffic control

Take full control of your machine, keep your eyes on the road ahead, and maintain optimal balance and speed.
