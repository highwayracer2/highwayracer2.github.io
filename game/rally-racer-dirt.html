ABOUT RALLY RACER DIRT

Rally Racer Dirt brings authentic drift rally racing straight to your browser! Drive real rally cars over dirt, gravel, mud, and snow tracks. Master high-speed power sliding, counter-steering, and weight transfer around loose dirt corners.

Compete in challenging rally stages, tune your vehicle's suspension and 4WD torque distribution, and climb the global rally leaderboards.

Key Features

  • Real Dirt Drift Physics: Authentic loose-gravel slide mechanics and counter-steering.
  • Custom Rally Cars: Unlock iconic rally cars with adjustable suspension and 4WD systems.
  • Varied Rally Surfaces: Race on dirt, mud, gravel, asphalt, and icy trails.

HOW TO PLAY & CONTROLS GUIDE

Initiate drift turns early before entering loose dirt corners to maintain momentum through the apex.

Controls & Keybindings

  • Steer & Drive: W, A, S, D or Arrow Keys.
  • Handbrake Slide: Spacebar.
  • Reset Track Position: R key.

Pro Tips

  • Feather the throttle during long gravel slides to keep the rally car from spinning out into ditch walls!
