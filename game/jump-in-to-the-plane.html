ABOUT JUMP IN TO THE PLANE

Jump In To The Plane is a spectacular stunt driving challenge where your goal is to launch a supercharged stunt car off a colossal sky ramp and land directly into the open cargo bay of a flying airplane!

Upgrade your car's engine power, jet boosters, and aerodynamics to gain maximum velocity and airtime trajectory required to hit the moving plane target.

Key Features

  • High-Flying Stunt Action: Launch cars into the sky towards a moving cargo plane.
  • Jet Thrusters: Engage rocket boosters mid-flight to fine-tune your landing glide.
  • Crazy Vehicle Upgrades: Maximize speed, jet fuel, and launch angle.

HOW TO PLAY & CONTROLS GUIDE

Keep your launch trajectory steady down the center of the ramp to line up directly with the cargo door.

Controls & Keybindings

  • Accelerate Ramp Run: Hold W, Up Arrow, or Spacebar.
  • Adjust Flight Pitch & Steer: A, D or Left / Right Arrow.
  • Fire Jet Boosters: Shift key.

Pro Tips

  • Trigger your rocket boosters in short bursts while airborne to align precisely with the cargo opening!
