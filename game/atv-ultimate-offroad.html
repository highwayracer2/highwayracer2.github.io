ABOUT ATV ULTIMATE OFFROAD

ATV Ultimate Offroad invites you to master quad bike riding across wild dirt tracks, rocky mountain sides, and muddy offroad trails! Experience raw 4x4 engine power as you conquer extreme slopes and perform daring jumps.

Compete in offroad tournaments, unlock powerful all-terrain vehicles, and customize your rider. Whether tackling steep hill climbs or drifting on gravel, ATV Ultimate Offroad tests your vehicle balance and driving prowess.

Key Features

  • Powerful Quad Bikes: Ride realistic 4x4 ATVs with high torque suspension.
  • Challenging Terrains: Race on mud, dirt, sand dunes, and mountain passes.
  • Stunt Ramps: Launch off natural cliffs and ramps for airborne stunts.
  • Free Ride & Race Modes: Explore open offroad maps or enter competitive cups.

HOW TO PLAY & CONTROLS GUIDE

Balance your ATV weight on uneven slopes to prevent flipping over during high-speed hill climbs.

Controls & Keybindings

  • Steer / Lean: W, A, S, D or Arrow Keys.
  • Accelerate: Up Arrow or W.
  • Brake: Down Arrow or S.
  • Nitro Boost: Shift or Spacebar.
  • Reset Position: R key.

Pro Tips

  • Lean backward while climbing steep hills to maintain rear-wheel traction.
  • Use short nitro bursts right after landing jumps to regain top acceleration quickly.
