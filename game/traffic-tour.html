ABOUT TRAFFIC TOUR - ULTIMATE HIGHWAY RACING

Traffic Tour is a brand new endless arcade racing game that takes you to another level of smooth driving simulations and high graphic quality. Designed for traffic racing fans, Traffic Tour offers an exhilarating highway driving experience through real-time traffic, featuring over 40 customizable cars, realistic vehicle cockpits, dynamic weather, and 5 distinct game modes!

Speed down infinite highway lanes, maneuver through dense traffic of cars and trucks, collect rewards, and upgrade your vehicle arsenal. Whether in Career mode or Endless Highway, Traffic Tour provides unmatched velocity directly in your web browser.

Key Game Features

  • 40+ Unique Cars: Unlock sports cars, muscle cars, hypercars, and SUVs with realistic engine sounds.
  • 5 Thrilling Game Modes: Career mode (100+ missions), Time Trial, Endless Highway, Two-Way, and Free Run.
  • 5 Environments: Highway, City, Desert, Rain, and Snow with dynamic Day & Night cycles.
  • 1st Person Cockpit View: Immersive cockpit camera with functional dashboard meters.

HOW TO PLAY & CONTROLS GUIDE

Mastering Traffic Tour requires sharp reflexes and strategic lane switching.

Controls & Keybindings

  • Steer Left / Right: Use W, A, S, D or Arrow Keys.
  • Accelerate: Press Up Arrow or W key.
  • Brake / Reverse: Press Down Arrow or S key.
  • Nitrous Boost: Press Shift or Spacebar.
  • Change Camera View: Press C key.

Pro Driving Tips

  • Overtake vehicles closely at speeds over 100 km/h to trigger close-call bonus points!
  • Driving against traffic flow in Two-Way mode doubles your score accumulation rate!
