ABOUT HIGHWAY RACER 2

Highway Racer 2 delivers the ultimate endless highway traffic driving experience! Weave through dense multi-lane traffic at speeds exceeding 200 km/h, performing close-call overtakes for bonus cash multiplier points.

Drive through dynamic day, night, rain, and desert highway maps. Choose between One-Way, Two-Way (opposite lane), Time Trial, and Speed Bomb game modes!

Key Features

  • Endless Highway Traffic: Weave past trucks, buses, and sedan traffic.
  • 4 Dynamic Game Modes: One-Way, Two-Way opposite flow, Time Trial, and Bomb Run.
  • Close-Call Bonuses: Overtake vehicles closely at high speeds for extra points.

HOW TO PLAY & CONTROLS GUIDE

Driving in the opposite traffic lane in Two-Way mode doubles your score accumulation rate!

Controls & Keybindings

  • Steer Left / Right: A, D or Left / Right Arrow.
  • Accelerate: W or Up Arrow.
  • Brake: S or Down Arrow.
  • Nitro Boost: Spacebar or Shift.

Pro Tips

  • Keep your speed above 100 km/h constantly to maintain your close-call point multiplier active!
