Frequently Asked Questions

Are all games on Highway Racer 2 completely free to play?

Yes, 100%! Every game hosted on Highway Racer 2 is completely free to enjoy directly in your web browser. You never need to purchase subscriptions, buy tokens, or download external software.

Do games work on school Chromebooks and office PCs?

All of our games run on modern HTML5, Canvas, and WebGL standards with lightweight memory footprints, making them ideal for Chromebooks, Macs, Windows laptops, and mobile devices without requiring Flash or administrative install privileges.

What should I do if a game stutters or lags?

Ensure Hardware Acceleration is toggled ON in your browser settings (Chrome/Edge: Settings > System > Use graphics acceleration when available). Closing unused background tabs or disabling battery-saver mode also substantially boosts WebGL FPS.

How can game developers publish their games on Highway Racer 2?

Indie developers can send playable HTML5 build links or zip archives to dev@highwayracer2.github.io with artwork, gameplay descriptions, and licensing terms. Our curation team will review and respond within 3 business days.
