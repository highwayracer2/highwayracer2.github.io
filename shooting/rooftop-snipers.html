HOW TO PLAY & CONTROLS GUIDE

Equip your favorite weapons, take advantage of tactical cover, and aim for critical hit zones to neutralize opponents swiftly.

Controls & Keybindings

  • Combat Controls: W to jump, E to shoot sniper rifle (Player 1); I to jump, O to shoot (Player 2).
  • Fullscreen Mode: Click the circular fullscreen icon at the bottom-right corner of the gameplay stage for an immersive experience.

Pro Combat Tips

  • Reload your weapon during pauses behind cover, and always keep your crosshair positioned at head-level for quick critical headshots!
