ABOUT GETAWAY SHOOTOUT

Getaway Shootout is a thrilling combat shooting game available to play free online directly in your browser with zero downloads. Race to the extraction helicopter while jumping, shooting, and shoving three rival escapees in hilarious chaotic 2D physics.

Lock in your focus, master your weapon handling, and test your combat reflexes across intense firefights and challenging shooting targets on Highway Racer 2.

HOW TO PLAY & CONTROLS GUIDE

Equip your favorite weapons, take advantage of tactical cover, and aim for critical hit zones to neutralize opponents swiftly.

Controls & Keybindings

  • Combat Controls: W / I to jump left, E / O to jump right, R / P to shoot weapon.
  • Fullscreen Mode: Click the circular fullscreen icon at the bottom-right corner of the gameplay stage for an immersive experience.

Pro Combat Tips

  • Reload your weapon during pauses behind cover, and always keep your crosshair positioned at head-level for quick critical headshots!
